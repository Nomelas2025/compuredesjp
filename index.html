Technology and Responsive Design

Technology is part of everyday life, from mobile devices and computers to wireless networks and cloud services. This page presents several examples of technologies that support communication, productivity, security, and access to information.

The site was designed with a mobile-first approach so that the content remains readable and useful on phones, tablets, and large screens. The layout also responds to user preferences, including reduced motion and dark color schemes, making the experience more accessible.
